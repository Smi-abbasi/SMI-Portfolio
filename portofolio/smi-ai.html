Project Overview

SMI AI is a personal AI assistant designed to combine modern language models with document intelligence and conversational AI. The project focuses on creating a fast, privacy-first assistant capable of answering questions, understanding uploaded documents, and assisting developers with programming, databases, and AI concepts.

Core Features

💬 AI Chat Assistant

Natural conversations powered by local Large Language Models.

📄 Document Understanding

Upload documents and ask questions using Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

🔍 Semantic Search

Retrieve relevant information through vector embeddings.

🔒 Privacy First

Runs locally using Ollama so your data remains private.
